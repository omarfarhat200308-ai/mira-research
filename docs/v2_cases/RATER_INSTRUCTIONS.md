# Instructions for clinician raters (MIRA v2)

You will see short messages like those a family member or community health worker might type on WhatsApp.
For each message, choose ONE tier based only on what is written:

* **Emergency**: needs a hospital or emergency care now (within about 1-2 hours).
* **Urgent**: should be seen at a health facility within about 24 hours.
* **Routine**: home care or a routine visit is reasonable.
* **Cannot_decide**: the message is too incomplete. In the last column write what you would ask first, and
  (if you wish) which tier you would use while waiting for the answer.

Please:
1. Rate alone, from the text only. Do not look at any software output. You will not be shown any.
2. Give a confidence from 1 (guess) to 5 (certain).
3. If a message is unrealistic, unclear or clinically wrong, say so in the last column; you may suggest a rewrite.
4. Return the sheet by the agreed date. Your ratings are used as submitted; disagreements between raters are kept and reported.

Some messages are in romanized Hindi or Telugu; if you are not comfortable in that language, write
"language" in the last column and leave the tier blank.

Your name, qualification and registration status will be listed in the paper only with your permission.
No patient data is involved: all messages are fictional.
